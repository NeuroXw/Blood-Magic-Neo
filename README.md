# Blood Magic: Neo
Unofficial NeoForge port of Blood Magic 1.20.1 for Minecraft 26.1.2.

This is a Beta community port. The goal is to bring the core mechanics of Blood Magic into the new 26.1.2 environment using NeoForge.

## Current Features
- **Blood Altar:** Tiers 1-5, rune detection, LP crafting, and a custom GUI.
- **Blood Orbs:** All 5 tiers, player binding, and cross-dimensional soul network.
- **Rituals:** 32 registered rituals (27 with active effects), including Water, Green Grove, and Feathered Knife.
- **Demon Will System:** 5 will types, chunk aura, crystal clusters, gems, catalysts, ritual configuration, and HUD gauge.
- **Sigils:** 14 utility sigils (Growth, Fast Miner, Magnetism, etc.).
- **Machines:** Alchemy Table, Hellfire Forge, ARC, Demon Crucible, Demon Crystallizer, and Demon Pylon.
- **Dungeon Blocks:** 135 decorative blocks.
- **Tools & Items:** Sacrificial Dagger, Alchemy Flasks, Activation Crystals, Ritual Diviner, and Ritual Tinkerer.

## Bug Reports & Feedback
If you encounter any bugs (e.g., crashes, GUI issues, or machines not working), please open an **Issue** on the Issues page. Please include your `latest.log` and steps to reproduce the problem.
